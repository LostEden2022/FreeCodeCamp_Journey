SHERPALOOZA

Join us for the first annual Sherpa Festival!

When: October 3, 2026

Where: Boulder Park

The 'Chham' Masked Dance & Sacred Storytelling

A spectacular evening event where monks from local monasteries perform sacred, masked "Chham" dances. This event brings to life the ancient legends of Buddhism’s triumph over malevolent spirits in the Himalayas, all set against a backdrop of a colossal, illuminated mandala.

  • The Monastic Procession: The event begins with a solemn, powerful procession of monks playing traditional instruments—long-horned dungchen, cymbals, and drums—as they escort the dancers from the monastery to the main festival ground.
  • Mandala Ritual & Lighting: Before the dance, a large, intricate sand mandala, painstakingly created over days, is consecrated and then dramatically illuminated by hundreds of traditional butter lamps, creating a sacred space.
  • Legend Recitation: During intervals between the dances, a respected Sherpa elder, acting as the festival storyteller, recites the specific legends and moral tales associated with each mask and dance, translating their meaning for all attendees.

The Khumbu Craftsmanship & Skills Showcase

This event is a celebration of the unique practical skills that define the Sherpa people. It moves beyond standard fair booths to offer a dynamic, interactive "village" where visitors can see master craftspeople and elite athletes demonstrate their expertise and even teach their skills.

  • The master weaver’s loom: An interactive station where Sherpa women demonstrate the entire process of making traditional woolen items, from hand-spinning yak wool to weaving the distinct, striped aprons (pangden) on a foot-powered backstrap loom.
  • Knot-tying & Rigging Clinic: Led by certified IFMGA Sherpa guides, this is a hands-on workshop where attendees learn essential mountaineering safety knots and the principles of rigging a fixed-line, a fundamental skill for high-altitude climbing.
  • Butter Sculpture Competition: A unique art event where local artists compete to create the most intricate, spiritual, or playful temporary sculptures using only colored yak butter and traditional wooden tools.

The 'Himalayan Strength' Sherpa Pentathlon

A friendly yet fierce athletic competition designed to test the distinct skills and remarkable physical endurance for which Sherpas are world-renowned. It is not a race to the summit, but a multi-disciplinary contest that honours their heritage as both farmers and elite expedition workers.

  • The 'Doko' Carry Endurance: Competitors must race on a challenging, uphill, multi-terrain circuit while carrying a traditional woven wicker basket (doko) filled with a standard weight (e.g., 30kg).
  • Precision Rope Coiling: A race to see who can perfectly coil a standard 60-meter climbing rope the fastest and neatest, a critical task for safety and efficiency on an expedition.
  • 'Chang' (Rice Beer) Toast & Song: The pentathlon concludes not on a field, but in a traditional Sherpa house (lhakhang). To gain final points, competitors must deliver a perfect traditional Sherpa toast and lead the crowd in a unison song of friendship and victory, judged on spirit and cultural accuracy.
