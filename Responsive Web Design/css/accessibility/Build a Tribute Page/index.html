Brigadier General Russell William Volckmann

"We were not stragglers hiding out to save our skins; we were an army operating behind enemy lines." — R.W. Volckmann

Profile and Key Information

Attribute Details

Rank Brigadier General (U.S. Army)
Born October 23, 1911 (Clinton, Iowa)

Key Legacy Co-founder/Architect of the U.S. Army Special Forces (Green Berets)

Chronological Timeline

  • West Point Graduation & Early Commission

    1934

    Graduated from West Point as a Second Lieutenant in the Infantry. Served in various stateside infantry assignments before being deployed to the Philippines in 1940.

  • Stationed in Baguio City

    1940–1941

    Assigned to Northern Luzon as an executive officer and senior instructor to the 11th Infantry Division of the Philippine Army, based near Baguio City.

  • Battle of Bataan & Escape to the Mountains

    1942

    Following the surrender order at Bataan, Major Volckmann and Capt. Donald Blackburn refused to surrender. They evaded Japanese patrols and slipped north into the Cordillera mountains around Baguio and Ifugao.

  • Formation of USAFIP-NL

    1942–1942

    Organized scattered American survivors and thousands of indigenous Filipino guerrillas into the United States Army Forces in the Philippines – Northern Luzon (USAFIP-NL), eventually commanding over 20,000 resistance fighters.

  • Liberation of Luzon & Yamashita's Fall

    1945

    Organized scattered American survivors and thousands of indigenous Filipino guerrillas into the United States Army Forces in the Philippines – Northern Luzon (USAFIP-NL), eventually commanding over 20,000 resistance fighters.

  • Fathering Special Forces Operations

    1950–1952

    Authored FM 31-21 (Organization and Conduct of Guerrilla Warfare) and worked closely with Col. Aaron Bank at the Psychological Warfare Center (Fort Bragg) to formally establish the U.S. Army Special Forces in 1952.
